# 🎥 OrderOnTheGo – Full App Demo

Welcome to the official demo of **OrderOnTheGo**, a full-stack MERN food ordering platform developed during our internship.

---

## 🚀 What You'll See in the Demo

This video demonstrates the complete user flow of the application:

- 🔐 Login / Signup
- 🍴 Browsing Restaurants & Menus
- 🛒 Adding Items to Cart
- ➕ Updating Quantity / Removing Items
- 💰 Real-time Price Calculation
- ✅ Order Placement
- 📱 Responsive Layout on All Devices
